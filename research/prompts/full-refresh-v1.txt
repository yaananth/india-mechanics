India Mechanics full refresh prompt, version 1

Target date: use the actual current date supplied by the runtime.

Refresh the complete published India Mechanics research record for India and
every published state jurisdiction. This is a publication workflow, not a
browser reload and not a machine-feed-only update.

Required lanes:

- current Prime Minister, Chief Ministers, governing parties, elections, and
  office-term dates;
- World Bank, V-Dem, and checked-in manual indicator series;
- Parliament bill register, official bill text explanations, laws, rules,
  judgments, and policy status;
- Union and state budgets, revised estimates, implementation, and fiscal risks;
- crime and public safety, including current NCRB publication availability and
  the post-July-1-2024 legal-classification break;
- trade agreements and implementation status;
- disasters, protests, social conflict, major achievements, major failures, and
  event accountability;
- policy, budget, PM, and CM rating evidence;
- source accessibility, provenance, reliability, limitations, and review dates.

Evidence rules:

1. Prefer controlling primary records.
2. Corroborate contested impact and government-performance claims with
   independent high-quality evidence.
3. Keep administrative claims, measured outcomes, estimates, models, and
   editorial judgments separate.
4. Do not infer a current fact from an old source, a placeholder year page, a
   machine feed, or social media.
5. Do not change a rating unless its component evidence changes. Never count the
   same outcome twice.
6. Preserve uncertainty, disagreements, legal status, attribution limits, and
   constitutional division of responsibility.

Publication requirements:

- save and validate bounded research batches;
- update sources before dependent records;
- add or update the corresponding ingestion batch;
- advance only cutoffs actually reviewed through the target date;
- list any stale or blocked lane explicitly;
- seed, audit, test, lint, build, check sources, and run bundled Browser E2E at
  desktop and mobile widths;
- commit, push, save a Sites version, deploy the canonical public site, and
  verify production APIs and visible cutoff text.

Return a concise refresh ledger containing:

- target date and resulting cutoffs;
- changed records;
- lanes reviewed with no publication change;
- stale or blocked lanes;
- validation results;
- commit and deployment status.
